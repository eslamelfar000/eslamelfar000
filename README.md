## Hi, I'm Eslam

I'm a front-end developer from Egypt. I build web apps with React, Next.js and TypeScript, mostly SaaS and LMS platforms, admin dashboards and online stores, and a good part of them in Arabic (RTL) as well as English.

I've been working full-time at 7P Marketing & Software Company since 2023 on the front ends of their SaaS products, and freelancing since 2022 for clients in Egypt and Kuwait.
